Open it in your browser

Every app works from a web browser on your phone, tablet or computer. Tap Open on any card.

2

Or put it on your home screen

On iPhone: Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android: the ⋮ menu, then Install app. It then opens like a real app.

3

Get the app, where there is one

Some apps have their own app for Android, the TV or Windows. Look for Get the app on the card. On Android, allow “install unknown apps” for your browser the first time.
